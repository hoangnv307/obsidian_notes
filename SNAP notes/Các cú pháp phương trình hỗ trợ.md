## Toán tử bit 
- `X || Y` hoặc `X or Y` Logical OR
- `X && Y` hoặc `X and Y` Logical AND

- `X == Y`
- `X!= Y`
- `X < Y`
- `X<=Y`
- `X > Y`
- `X >= Y`
